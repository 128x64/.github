# 128x64 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

128x64 is committed to maintaining a professional, welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for everyone participating in our projects and official community spaces.

We expect all contributors, members, maintainers, administrators, and community participants to act responsibly and treat others with respect.

This Code of Conduct applies throughout the 128x64 organization.

## 1. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Be constructive when providing feedback.
- Respect differing opinions and perspectives.
- Accept reasonable criticism and review feedback.
- Collaborate in good faith.
- Respect the work, time, and contributions of others.
- Follow repository guidelines and organization policies.
- Help maintain a welcoming and productive environment.

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are prohibited:

### Harassment and Abuse
- Harassment, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Unwanted or abusive contact.

### Disruptive Conduct
- Spam or repeated irrelevant messages.
- Deliberate disruption of projects or discussions.
- Trolling or intentionally hostile behavior.
- Repeatedly derailing productive conversations.

### Malicious Activity
- Deliberate attempts to damage repositories or projects.
- Unauthorized access attempts.
- Malicious code or intentionally harmful contributions.
- Impersonation or deceptive activity.
- Sharing private or confidential information without authorization.

### Misuse of Organization Resources
- Unauthorized use of organization accounts or permissions.
- Sharing restricted repository contents without permission.
- Misrepresenting oneself as an authorized 128x64 representative.
- Circumventing established organization policies.

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official 128x64 spaces and activities, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- GitHub Issues.
- GitHub Pull Requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Organization-managed documentation.
- Official 128x64 communication channels.
- Other spaces explicitly operated or managed by 128x64.

Organization members are expected to follow applicable company policies when acting in an official 128x64 capacity.

This Code of Conduct is not intended to regulate members' unrelated personal lives.

## 5. Enforcement

The organization owner and authorized administrators are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

The standard enforcement process uses three strikes:

### First Strike — Formal Warning

The individual receives a warning explaining the behavior that violated the Code of Conduct and the changes expected.

### Second Strike — Removal of Contribution Privileges

The individual may lose permission to contribute to 128x64 repositories and projects.

This may include restrictions on submitting or participating in contributions.

### Third Strike — Report to GitHub

The individual may be reported to GitHub for further review, and 128x64 may take additional appropriate action.

### Serious Violations

The three-strike process is a standard escalation procedure, not a requirement to tolerate serious misconduct.

Severe incidents, threats, security issues, or conduct that violates GitHub policies may be reported immediately and may result in immediate access restrictions or removal.

GitHub reports will be made when appropriate under GitHub's policies and reporting procedures.

## 6. Administrator Authority

The organization owner and authorized administrators are responsible for:

- Reviewing reports.
- Investigating alleged violations.
- Determining appropriate enforcement actions.
- Applying organization-level restrictions.
- Communicating decisions where appropriate.

Enforcement decisions should be made fairly, consistently, and with consideration of the available evidence.

## 7. Confidentiality

Reports and related evidence should not be publicly disclosed without a legitimate reason.

We will make reasonable efforts to protect the identity and privacy of individuals involved.

## 8. Acknowledgment

By participating in official 128x64 spaces and activities, individuals are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

We appreciate everyone who helps maintain a respectful, safe, and productive community.
