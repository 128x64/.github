# 128x64 Governance

This document describes the organizational structure, administrative responsibilities, decision-making process, and maintainer expectations of 128x64.

These guidelines apply across the organization and its projects.

## 1. Organization Structure

128x64 is managed as one organization with multiple divisions and projects.

The divisions operate under the broader organization structure.

Individual repositories may have their own maintainers, development processes, and technical requirements, provided they remain consistent with organization-wide policies.

## 3. Administrator Responsibilities

Authorized administrators may:

- Manage organization repositories and settings within their permissions.
- Appoint maintainers.
- Remove maintainer permissions.
- Review membership requests.
- Manage access to organization resources.
- Enforce organization policies.
- Assist with community moderation.
- Review and address administrative matters.

The organization owner retains final authority over organization-wide decisions.

## 4. Repository Maintainers

Maintainers are individuals authorized to oversee one or more repositories.

Their responsibilities may include:

- Reviewing Pull Requests.
- Reviewing and organizing Issues.
- Maintaining documentation.
- Helping contributors.
- Monitoring project quality.
- Ensuring repository policies are followed.
- Coordinating development work.
- Making repository-level decisions within their assigned authority.

Maintainers do not automatically receive organization-wide administrative authority.

## 5. Repository-Level Decisions

Maintainers and authorized organization members may make decisions within repositories they are responsible for.

Examples include:

- Implementation choices.
- Bug prioritization.
- Documentation improvements.
- Development workflows.
- Repository-specific coding conventions.
- Release preparation.

The organization owner may review or change decisions when necessary.

Organization-wide policies and decisions take precedence over repository-level preferences.

## 6. Appointment and Removal of Maintainers

Any of the four recognized organization administrators may appoint or remove maintainers.

Maintainer appointments should consider:

- Technical ability and relevant experience.
- Reliability.
- Quality of contributions.
- Communication and collaboration.
- Understanding of organization policies.
- Ability to responsibly manage repository permissions.

Maintainer permissions may be changed or revoked when necessary.

## 7. Organization Membership

128x64 welcomes individuals interested in becoming organization members.

Contributors may request membership by contacting:

raphaelcvarghese@gmail.com

Membership requests are reviewed by the organization administrators.

Factors considered may include:

- Contribution history.
- Reliability and consistency.
- Technical or professional ability.
- Collaboration and communication.
- Understanding of organization policies.
- Organizational needs.

Membership is not automatic and is subject to approval.

Organization membership and repository permissions are separate matters. Access should be granted according to an individual's responsibilities.

## 8. Policy Compliance

All organization members, administrators, and maintainers are expected to follow applicable 128x64 policies while acting in an official organizational capacity.

These include:

- SECURITY.md
- CONTRIBUTING.md
- CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md
- Applicable repository LICENSE files.
- Other official organization policies.

Members are not expected to apply workplace policies to unrelated activities in their personal lives.

## 9. Policy Changes

Organization-wide policies may be reviewed and updated by the organization owner.

Administrators may propose changes and assist with implementation.

The organization owner has final authority over the adoption of organization-wide policies.

## 10. Final Authority

The organization owner is the final decision-maker for organization-wide governance and administrative matters.

Where disagreements cannot be resolved at the repository or administrator level, the organization owner makes the final determination.
