## Organization-wide repositories

These repositories serve all of 128x64 and are not owned by an individual division:

- [.github](https://github.com/128x64/.github) — shared GitHub policies, community health files, and organization automation.
- [Discussions](https://github.com/128x64/Discussions) — organization-wide discussions.

## Ownership and governance

- Division ownership identifies the primary organizational home for a repository; it does not transfer repository ownership away from the 128x64 GitHub organization.
- Organization-wide policies in the [.github repository](https://github.com/128x64/.github) apply across divisions.
- The organization owner retains final organization-wide decision authority under [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
- Repository assignments should be updated here when projects are created, transferred between divisions, or made organization-wide.

## Next steps

Division-specific project descriptions, roadmaps, maintainers, and team permissions will be documented as they are decided. No maintainers or team permissions are implied by the repository assignments above.
